Out-of-State / Remote Casting

Any Network / 4G/5G

How to cast video or mirror your screen from another city/state when not on home Wi-Fi:

1. Instant Cloud Video Caster (Built-in)
Use the Play Video or Stream box above! Copy any YouTube video or live satsang link on your phone anywhere in the world and tap Cast. It plays instantly on the TV over cloud!
2. YouTube "Watch on TV" Code (WAN Cast)
On TV YouTube app → Settings → Link with TV Code. Enter the 12-digit code in your phone's YouTube app once. After that, your father's TV permanently shows up on your phone's Cast button from anywhere!
3. Out-of-State Laptop Screen Mirroring
To mirror your full laptop/phone screen across states, use Tailscale Mesh VPN (makes phone & TV behave as if on the same living room Wi-Fi) or RustDesk for instant remote desktop display.
