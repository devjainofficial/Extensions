ChromaScope Test Playground

Test pixel picking, live element hover inspection, gradient detection, and chromesthesia tones.

🎯 Key Features & Testing Instructions:
  1. Open Extension Shortcut: Press Ctrl+Shift+X anywhere in Edge to instantly open the ChromaScope popup!
  2. Live Page Loupe & Hover Inspector: Press Alt+Shift+C to launch the in-page magnified loupe overlay.
  3. Quick Screen EyeDropper: Press Alt+Shift+P to pick any pixel anywhere on your screen.
  4. Testing Hover Color Inspection: Hover your mouse over the interactive buttons below:
    • Notice how the loupe magnifies the live rendered pixel as it changes color on hover!
    • Press H while hovering: this locks the Hover State Comparison in the HUD, showing both the Normal (Resting) Color and Hover Color side-by-side with lightness delta and transition timing!
  5. Chromesthesia Sound Synthesizer: Pick a pixel or click the ♫ Play Tone button in the popup to hear the harmonic musical frequency of the color!
